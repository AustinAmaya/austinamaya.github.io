Calder

A single-user, personal calendar assistant.

Calder is a personal assistant built for one person, Austin. It runs on Austin's own home server and helps him manage his Google Calendar and Google Tasks: reading his calendars, planning his day, preparing a daily brief, and creating or updating events and tasks when he asks.

What Calder does with Google data

  • Reads Austin's calendar events and task lists so it can brief him and plan his time.
  • Creates and edits calendars, events and tasks in Austin's own Google account, at his request.
  • Talks to Austin through his private messaging app.

Who can use it

Only Austin. Calder is not a public service, has no other users, and offers no sign-up. Google Calendar and Google Tasks data is read and written only on Austin's own server. It is never shared with anyone, never sold, and never used for advertising.
